## Database and release boundary

The additive `20261015225832_public_inquiries` migration must be applied before the new handlers serve traffic. The table is server-owned, has row-level security and no browser/API-role table grants. The application resolves public tenant ownership itself; organization admins can read only their organization's records. The admin inbox is bounded and offers no conversion/approval actions. Inquiry receipt and email delivery are distinct facts; failures remain visible and retries do not overwrite a different submission under the same identity.

The migration and privilege/uniqueness/retention behavior were tested against disposable local PostgreSQL, not production. No production data/configuration, published story record, or external email was changed during preparation. Source-level publication withholding protects older story rows without a production content rewrite.
