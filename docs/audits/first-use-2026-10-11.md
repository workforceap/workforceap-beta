## Submission retry follow-up

The pre-screening submit handler previously returned Already submitted for
every repeat. A lost success response therefore appeared to be a failed
submission; two concurrent initial reads could also leave one caller with a
unique-row error. The separate retry repair acknowledges an existing success
only for the signed-in member when all normalized screening answers,
`User.phone` and `Profile.profileAddress` exactly match. Omitted optional
`hearAboutOther` is compared as null, matching the existing write rule.
Changed/invalid repeats still fail, and missing or subsequently edited
contact fields conservatively require refreshing the member's current state.

Only a `P2002` for the response insert's single user-ID unique key triggers
a same-member reread after transaction rollback. Unrelated unique errors,
downstream profile failures and a missing authoritative response remain
errors. Retry acknowledgements do not rewrite answers, contacts or eligibility,
delete another draft, enqueue a staff email or emit a new audit. The original
completed-preassessment requirement and the winner's existing transactional
writes/notification/audit remain unchanged.

The [real-handler retry suite](../../tests/api/member-pre-screening-idempotency.spec.ts)
passes 17 cases, including mismatching concurrent answers/contacts, caller
identity fields, null/changed contacts and unrelated unique failures. Together
with the transaction/rollback and distinct WIOA-screening boundary checks in
[eligibility writers](../../tests/api/eligibility-writers.spec.ts), the focused
group passes 41 cases. Concurrent unique conflicts use a controlled Prisma
fixture that waits for the winner's saved state; this is not real PostgreSQL
concurrency or RLS verification. Optional draft recovery is a separate change.
